# Start here — build your synthetic self today

> You're about to build a personal AI agent that knows you and helps you — with *doing* (projects, money, health) or *being* (wellbeing, presence, growth), or both. First version: ~30–45 minutes, **free**.
>
> There are two choices to make, and they're independent: a **path** (what it's *for*) and a **build** (where you *make* it). Find your combo in the grid, then follow that one recipe. You don't read the whole kit — you read your cell.

---

## Step 1 — Find your combo (1 min)

Two questions: **doing or being?** (the row) and **no files, or files?** (the column).

|  | **No-code — Gemini**  *(free, no files)* | **Maker — Cowork · Cursor · Claude**  *(agent keeps its own memory)* |
|---|---|---|
| **Operator** — *help me do* | **① Operator · No-code** | **② Operator · Maker** |
| **Companion** — *help me be* | **③ Companion · No-code** | **④ Companion · Maker** |

- **Row — Operator vs. Companion:** Operator ranks your day and names your drift (the `synth-shalom` shape); Companion tends your wellbeing and witnesses your growth (a personal Cosmo). Unsure or wary of upkeep? → **Companion**. Need to ship? → **Operator**. (Deeper: `PATHS.md`.)
- **Column — No-code vs. Maker:** No-code is a Gemini **Gem**, zero files. Maker is a **folder** an agent reads *and writes*, so it keeps its own memory and can read your calendar. Unsure? → **No-code** (faster; your docs move to Maker later unchanged).

Now scroll to your number below. That's your whole recipe.

## Step 2 — Your recipe

> Every recipe is the same five lines: your **brief**, your **brain docs**, your **setup guide**, your **first words**, and the **one gotcha** for that combo. Fill the `[[brackets]]` in each doc with your real answers as you go (`identity` matters most — it's what makes the agent *you*). Stuck on a doc? Paste it into any AI and say *"interview me to fill this in."*

### ① Operator · No-code (Gemini)
*Run my day. No files, free.*
1. **Brief:** paste `paths/operator/brief.md` into a new Gem's **Instructions**.
2. **Brain docs** (as Google Docs in a Drive folder): `identity`, `goals`, `practice`, `log`.
3. **Setup guide:** `setup/build-no-code-gemini.md`.
4. **First words:** *"check in with me."*
5. **The one gotcha:** a Gem reads but can't write — paste the check-in it hands you back into your `log` Doc. That paste *is* the memory.

### ② Operator · Maker (Cowork · Cursor · Claude)
*Run my day; the agent keeps its own memory and reads my real plate.*
1. **Brief:** rename `paths/operator/brief.md` → `AGENTS.md` at your folder root.
2. **Brain docs** (markdown in the folder): `identity`, `goals`, `practice`, `log`.
3. **Loops:** install `paths/operator/loops/` (check-in · review · spar · retro).
4. **Setup guide:** `setup/build-maker.md`.
5. **First words:** *"check in with me."* **Gotcha:** connect Calendar/Tasks **read-only** so it reads your real plate; it writes the `log` itself.

### ③ Companion · No-code (Gemini)
*Tend me. Lowest upkeep, free.*
1. **Brief:** paste `paths/companion/brief.md` into a new Gem's **Instructions**.
2. **Brain docs** (as Google Docs): `identity`, `teachers`, `log`. (`practice` optional.)
3. **Setup guide:** `setup/build-no-code-gemini.md`.
4. **First words:** *"attune with me."*
5. **The one gotcha:** a Gem can't fetch Cosmo live — the brief's built-in Cosmo distillation is enough. Paste witness-journal lines it gives you back into your `log` Doc.

### ④ Companion · Maker (Cowork · Cursor · Claude)
*Tend me — the richest version; the agent keeps the journal and stands on the live Cosmo.*
1. **Brief:** rename `paths/companion/brief.md` → `AGENTS.md` at your folder root.
2. **Brain docs** (markdown): `identity`, `teachers`, `log`.
3. **Loops:** install `paths/companion/loops/` (attune · reflect · discern · synthesize · witness).
4. **Setup guide:** `setup/build-maker.md` + `paths/companion/cosmo-grounding.md`.
5. **First words:** *"attune with me."* **Gotcha:** have it fetch Cosmo's full constitution at session start (see `cosmo-grounding.md`); it keeps the witness journal itself.

> **Want both paths?** Give your agent both briefs and install both loop sets — they interleave. See *Blending* in `PATHS.md`. (Pick one build first; blending paths is independent of which build you're on.)

## Step 3 — Run it, then make it stick

**Run your first loop now** — Operator: *"check in with me"*; Companion: *"attune with me."* A good one sounds like a wiser, more honest version of you. If it sounds generic, your `identity` doc needs more real specifics.

**Make it a habit before you make it proactive.** Use it by hand for a week or two. *Only then* turn on a scheduled nudge (Scheduled Actions on No-code; a scheduled task on Maker). The rule, from `synth-shalom`: **proactivity is earned** — a ping before the thing is useful just becomes a notification you mute. (Companion: keep it especially light — a weekly nudge, not a daily demand.)

---

## What "good" looks like after a month

- **Operator:** you open the day knowing the one thing that matters, not a wall of 40 tasks; it's caught you drifting at least once and you were glad; your `log` holds a few decisions with the *why* preserved.
- **Companion:** you feel *met*, not managed; it's brought you out of your head and into your body more than once; it's reflected back growth you'd have discounted; and it's asked, at least once, whether it's still serving you — and meant it.

---

### A few honest cautions
- **It's a partner, not an authority.** It prepares and reflects; you decide and act. For **health and money** it organizes and surfaces tradeoffs — not a doctor, therapist, or financial advisor; for real decisions it points you to a qualified human.
- **Garbage in, garbage out.** A vague `identity` makes a generic agent. Specifics make it yours.
- **Keep it small.** The whole system should be readable in a few minutes. A lean synthetic self you use beats an elaborate one you don't — and on the Companion path, low-maintenance is the whole point.
