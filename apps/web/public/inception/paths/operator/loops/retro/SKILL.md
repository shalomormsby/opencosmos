# /retro — am I getting better?

You are running the retro loop — the **honest mirror**. Plain reporting, no flattery, no catastrophizing. A retro only works if the record is true. Cadence: weekly, or after something big. Read `AGENTS.md` and `brain/goals.md` first.

## Step 1 — establish the window
Read the most recent retro in `brain/log.md`. That date starts the window. If it's the first retro, use the last 7 days and say so.

## Step 2 — gather the trail
From `brain/log.md` and wherever the work lives:
- **What got done** in the window (shipped work, completed tasks, decisions made).
- **What was committed** — the prior retro's one improvement; any goal targets due in the window.
- **The check-ins** — what did they recommend, and were the recommendations acted on?

## Step 3 — write the retro
Append to `brain/log.md` (Retros). Cover:
- **Moved** — per goal: moved / partial / not at all, with evidence.
- **Slipped** — commitments that didn't land, stated plainly. Include anything aging past its date.
- **Drift** — work that mapped to no goal.
- **Where energy actually went** vs. the stated priority order. Name the gap: a starving top goal or a no-goal item eating the week is the finding.
- **Patterns + friction tally** — anything seen before. Keep a running count. **A friction seen in 2+ retros is structural.**
- **Prior improvement — adopted?** Honestly: adopted / rejected (why) / ignored (why). No scolding.
- **The one improvement** — exactly one concrete, adoptable-this-week change. Smaller is better.

## Step 4 — make it compound (the engine)
- **Friction escalation (fires at the 2nd occurrence).** If a friction has now appeared in 2+ retros, propose escalating it: a new target/goal in `brain/goals.md`, or a sharpened growth edge in `brain/identity.md`. Surface the proposed edit; the human ratifies.
- **Bet resolution.** For any logged decision whose "revisit" condition arrived, record whether the bet paid off — calibrating judgment against reality.

## Rules
- One improvement. The discipline is the constraint. If three things are worth fixing, pick the highest-leverage and name the rest as candidates for next time.
- Honest mirror: if the week was light or a goal didn't move, say so plainly. A thin week truthfully recorded beats a padded one.
