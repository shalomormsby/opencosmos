# Goals — what I'm aiming at

> Your current priorities, in **priority order** (the order matters — your agent uses it to break ties). Each goal needs three things: an **ambition** (why it matters, traced to your north star), **measurable targets** (so "progress" isn't a feeling), and the **live work** tied to it (so the agent can connect today's tasks to the goal they serve).
>
> Keep this to **3–5 goals.** More than five and nothing is a priority. Revisit every quarter; date your revisions.
>
> *These are this year's expressions of your [north star](identity.md). When you need the deepest "why" behind a priority, read up one level.*
