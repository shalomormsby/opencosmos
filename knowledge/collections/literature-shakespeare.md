# The Shakespeare Collection

> "All the world's a stage, and all the men and women merely players."

This collection organizes the vast output of William Shakespeare into a single, navigable hub within the OpenCosmos knowledge base. Shakespeare's work is foundational not just for literature, but for philosophical inquiry into the human condition.
